Psicosfera–Tecnósfera

Interdisciplinary Research · Computational Social Systems · Territorial Intelligence

¿Cómo interactúan los procesos humanos, las relaciones sociales, las instituciones y las tecnologías en la evolución de sistemas territoriales complejos?

How do human processes, social relations, institutions and technologies interact in the evolution of complex territorial systems?

Posicionamiento disciplinario. Esta línea de investigación surge desde los estudios territoriales, la sostenibilidad y la ciencia de sistemas. La Psicosfera se utiliza aquí como una dimensión analítica para estudiar procesos humanos y sociales; no pretende sustituir a la Psicología ni constituir una teoría psicológica independiente. El propósito es generar una interfaz interdisciplinaria que permita integrar aportes de Psicología, Ciencias Sociales, Ciencia de Redes, Inteligencia Artificial, estudios territoriales y otras disciplinas interesadas en comprender sistemas humanos complejos.
Disciplinary positioning. This research line emerges from territorial studies, sustainability and systems science. The Psychosphere is used here as an analytical dimension for studying human and social processes; it does not seek to replace Psychology or constitute an independent psychological theory. The purpose is to provide an interdisciplinary interface through which contributions from Psychology, Social Sciences, Network Science, Artificial Intelligence, territorial studies and related fields can be integrated.

Una línea de investigación interdisciplinaria

Esta línea estudia la interacción entre dos dimensiones analíticas de los sistemas territoriales.

La Psicosfera comprende procesos humanos, cognitivos, relacionales y sociales que intervienen en la percepción, interpretación y acción.

La Tecnósfera comprende estructuras materiales, tecnológicas, organizacionales e institucionales que pueden posibilitar, condicionar o restringir determinadas formas de acción.

El interés no está en estudiar estas dimensiones de manera aislada, sino en comprender las configuraciones que emergen de su interacción.

El propósito no es explicar lo humano únicamente desde la tecnología, ni explicar los territorios únicamente desde el comportamiento. Es estudiar las relaciones entre ambos dominios.

Problema de investigación

Los sistemas sociales territoriales difícilmente pueden comprenderse mediante una sola dimensión de análisis.

Infraestructura, tecnología, instituciones, recursos, conocimiento, relaciones sociales, percepciones y capacidades humanas interactúan de manera situada y pueden producir resultados diferentes incluso frente a condiciones aparentemente similares.

Por ello, la pregunta central es:

¿Cómo se configuran y transforman las relaciones entre procesos humanos, estructuras tecnológicas e instituciones en sistemas territoriales complejos?

Esta perspectiva permite conectar sostenibilidad, estudios territoriales, ciencias sociales, Psicología, ciencia de redes y herramientas computacionales sin reducir una disciplina a otra.

Preguntas de investigación

RQ1 · Representación

¿Cómo pueden representarse computacionalmente actores, capacidades, relaciones, proyectos y condiciones tecnosféricas que participan en sistemas sociales territoriales?

RQ2 · Exploración

¿Pueden las herramientas de análisis de redes, procesamiento semántico e inteligencia artificial ayudar a identificar relaciones potenciales o patrones que posteriormente puedan ser examinados mediante investigación empírica?

RQ3 · Cambio

¿Cómo se modifican las configuraciones relacionales y determinadas dimensiones del comportamiento o la acción colectiva después de intervenciones específicas?

RQ4 · Validación

¿Qué tipo de evidencia permite distinguir una asociación, una relación funcional o una correlación de un mecanismo causal propiamente tal?

Resultados de la investigación doctoral

El marco tiene como antecedente una investigación doctoral sobre sostenibilidad urbana en ciudades intermedias chilenas.

El estudio consideró 80 ciudades dentro del rango definido para la investigación y desarrolló una caracterización mediante cuatro arquetipos de Resiliencia–Transformabilidad (R-T).

Los casos estudiados en profundidad fueron:

  • Copiapó
  • Talcahuano
  • Valdivia
  • Angol

El período analizado corresponde a 2005–2023.

La investigación permitió explorar cómo distintas configuraciones territoriales pueden presentar combinaciones diferenciadas de resiliencia y transformabilidad.

La hipótesis interpretativa desarrollada en la investigación sostiene que estos atributos pueden comprenderse como propiedades emergentes de configuraciones históricas y co-evolutivas entre dimensiones tecnológicas, institucionales, sociales y humanas.

An Interdisciplinary Research Line

This research line studies the interaction between two analytical dimensions of territorial systems.

The Psychosphere refers to human, cognitive, relational and social processes involved in perception, interpretation and action.

The Technosphere refers to material, technological, organizational and institutional structures that may enable, condition or constrain particular forms of action.

The focus is not on studying these dimensions separately, but on understanding the configurations that emerge from their interaction.

The purpose is neither to explain human processes through technology alone nor to explain territories through behavior alone. It is to study the relationships between both domains.

Research Problem

Territorial social systems are difficult to understand through a single analytical dimension.

Infrastructure, technology, institutions, resources, knowledge, social relations, perceptions and human capabilities interact in situated ways and may produce different outcomes even under apparently similar conditions.

The central question is therefore:

How are relationships between human processes, technological structures and institutions configured and transformed in complex territorial systems?

Research Questions

RQ1 · Representation

How can actors, capabilities, relations, projects and technospheric conditions involved in territorial social systems be represented computationally?

RQ2 · Exploration

Can network analysis, semantic processing and artificial intelligence tools help identify potential relations or patterns that can later be examined through empirical research?

RQ3 · Change

How do relational configurations and selected dimensions of behavior or collective action change following specific interventions?

RQ4 · Validation

What kind of evidence allows us to distinguish association, functional relation or correlation from an actual causal mechanism?

Interdisciplinary Analytical Framework

The Psychosphere is used as an analytical dimension for representing human and social processes relevant to action.

It is not intended as an independent psychological theory, nor as a substitute for concepts developed within the specialized disciplines of the human sciences.

The Technosphere represents material, technical, organizational and institutional structures that may enable, condition or constrain particular forms of action.

Psychosphere Technosphere

These categories are analytical and open. Their specific meaning depends on the research problem and on the discipline used to study it.

The analytical focus lies in relationships between dimensions, rather than assuming that either dimension can explain the system on its own.

Interdisciplinary Relations

The framework is intended to function as an interdisciplinary interface.

  • Psychology: concepts and methods for studying individual, interpersonal, social and community processes.
  • Sociology: structures, relationships and collective processes.
  • Network Science: representation and analysis of relational configurations.
  • Computational Science: information structuring, processing and exploration.
  • Territorial Studies: spatial, institutional and historical contextualization.
  • Sustainability: a field of problems in which these dimensions can be examined together.

The framework does not seek to replace specialized disciplines; it seeks to create conditions for them to interact within a common representation of the system.

Doctoral Research Results

The framework has its antecedent in doctoral research on urban sustainability in Chilean intermediate cities.

The study considered 80 cities within the defined research range and developed a characterization based on four Resilience–Transformability (R-T) archetypes.

The in-depth cases were:

  • Copiapó
  • Talcahuano
  • Valdivia
  • Angol

The analyzed period was 2005–2023.

The research explored how different territorial configurations may exhibit differentiated combinations of resilience and transformability.

The interpretive hypothesis developed in the research proposes that these attributes can be understood as emergent properties of historical and co-evolutionary configurations involving technological, institutional, social and human dimensions.

A Continuation of the Research

The Psychosphere–Technosphere line represents a methodological development of this previous work.

The doctoral research examined territorial configurations mainly at a macro scale.

The new line seeks to explore with greater resolution the relationships between actors, capabilities, institutions, projects and human processes, incorporating computational representation and network analysis.

  • Which phenomena require direct observation?
  • How can semantic similarity be distinguished from substantive relation?

  • How can relationships be represented without exposing personal or sensitive information?
  • Which psychological concepts can appropriately operationalize the human processes represented by the Psychosphere?
  • Which research designs can integrate qualitative, quantitative, relational and territorial data?
